Privacy Policy

The Fan Edit Fan Club Collection is a free, static website. It has no accounts of its own and no server-side database.

What is stored

Only your own stars and watch history (which files you starred and when you last opened them). By default this lives only in your browser's local storage on your device.

Optional Google sync

If you choose Sync My List and sign in with Google, the same data is saved to a single file (fefc-userdata.json) in your own Google Drive app data folder, which is private to this app and to you. We request only the drive.appdata scope plus your basic profile (name, email, picture) to show who is signed in. We cannot see the rest of your Drive.

Sync My List on TVs and other devices

If you link a device with a Google TV code, a QR code or a 6-digit sync code, your stars, watch history and playback times are also stored on our server (Cloudflare KV), under a random sync ID or your Google account ID, so your devices can share them. Google sign-in on a TV only asks for your basic profile (name, email, picture). Sync codes and QR links expire after a few minutes. Signing out removes this device's link; email us if you want the stored list deleted.

What we don't do

Nothing is stored on our servers unless you turn on sync as described above. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. You can sign out at any time, and you can delete the app data from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps).
